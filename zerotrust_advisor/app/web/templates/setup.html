Router firmware and menu layout drift over time — treat this as a starting point, not gospel.

  1. Firewall logging. On the rules you want visibility into (especially inter-VLAN rules), enable per-rule logging and point syslog at {{ router_target_host }}:{{ config.syslog_port }} (UDP).

  3. Set Allowed source IPs in Settings to your router's IP, or messages will be received but rejected (see the counts above).

Read-only by default. Nothing is ever written back to UniFi from this add-on today, regardless of the apply-mode setting further down.
